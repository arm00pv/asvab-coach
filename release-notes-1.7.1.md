# v1.7.1 - the spoken study guide

Every screen in the app can now explain itself out loud.

* **A speaker beside every title.** Tap it and the screen tells you what it is,
  what the numbers on it mean, and what to do next. Tap it again to stop, or
  leave the screen and it stops with you.
* **Eleven written scripts**, one per screen, built from the same numbers the
  screen shows - the plan's day count, the report's percentile, the review's
  missed-and-blank tally. The guide cannot say something the screen is not
  saying.
* **Hands free.** In Settings, switch on *Read each screen as it opens* and the
  guide starts talking by itself as you move between tabs and lessons.
* **Your device's own voice.** No download, no connection needed, nothing
  recorded and nothing sent anywhere. Android's text-to-speech engine on the
  phone, the browser's on the web build.
* **Language and voice, named.** Settings shows the language and the voice, and
  the list behind it has one row per language and region - English (United
  States), English (United Kingdom), English (India) - chosen from the voices
  installed on the device. The guide is written in English today, and the list
  is limited to what is written, so a voice can never read words it cannot
  pronounce.
* **Speaking speed.** Slower, normal, quicker - with a *Hear it* button so you
  know what you are choosing before you commit.
* **AI tutor: stated, not faked.** Settings carries an honest section on the
  on-device tutor: what it will do, which model it will use, and why it is not in
  this build (a 1.33 GB separate download, Android only).

Found on the phone and fixed before release:

* the voice list printed every installed engine voice as a pill - fifty-two
  names like "en-au-x-auc-local" - which pushed the switch that matters off the
  screen. It now counts them and offers one row per language and region, and the
  chosen voice is named to the engine, so the pick is real rather than a
  gesture.
* returning to a tab from a pushed screen left it silent, because a tab never
  re-mounts. The guide now hears the pop and speaks again.

Nothing about scoring, jobs or the question bank changed.
