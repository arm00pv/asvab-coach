As an MP myself I wouldn't recommend it (I fucking hate it), the job itself is not bad for the National Guard but it can be either lackluster or just a pain in the ass. I would say some people enjoy being an MP with at least half disliking if not outright hating or being completely incompetent at the job.

If you want guaranteed and legit law enforcement training and experience join a city police department or state troopers.

I was mobilized for the Memphis Safe Task Force and met only a handful of MP's that were actually high speed and humble about their job and they were actually cops and SWAT members in their civilian career. But I've met and been under people who thought they were "tacticool" or "just like the infantry" but are either to pussy for actual violence with armed gunmen or are incompetent as hell when deciding tactics and procedures.

So if you're going into a combat MOS be it infantry, Combat Engineers, Cav Scouts, and etc. Don't be slapped ass and be eager to learn, know when to shut up, and be competent at the basics. The biggest thing to avoid is having an ego about yourself as I've witnessed Infantry dudes have that arrogant mindset.

Best of luck.
