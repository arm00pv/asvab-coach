s The Armed Services Vocational Aptitude Battery (ASVAB) is the primary assessment used by the U.S. military to evaluate candidates for enlistment. The exam consists of multiple subtests, fo
ur of which form the Armed Forces Qualification Test (AFQT). The AFQT is not a separate test from the ASVAB, but rather a subsection used to determine basic eligibility for enlistment across
 all branches. The four subtests that make up the AFQT are: Arithmetic Reasoning (AR) Mathematics Knowledge (MK) Paragraph Comprehension (PC) Word Knowledge (WK) The ASVAB offers two version
s: a computerized test known as the CAT-ASVAB (Computerized Adaptive Test) or a traditional, paper-and-pencil format. The AFQT is a main component of both versions of the exam. Our AFQT cour
se is a preview of our comprehensive ASVAB course and is designed to help you prepare specifically for the branch qualification subtests. The ASVAB exam is hosted by ASVAB Enlistment Testing

basic competencies required for military service. Scores from these sections are converted and reported as a percentile between 1 and 99 using this formula: AFQT = 2 × (VE) + AR + MK where V
E (Verbal Expression) = WK + PC Percentiles represent how an examinee performed relative to a nationally representative sample. For example, a score of 40 indicates that you scored as well o
r better than 40% of the reference group. Scores are also divided into the following performance categories: Category I: 93 - 99 Category II: 65 - 92 Category IIIA: 50 - 64 Category IIIB: 31
 - 49 Category IV: 10 - 30 Category V: 1 - 9 (not qualified) AFQT scores are used by military branches to determine eligibility, with each branch setting its own minimum score required to en
list. According to official military websites, the minimum qualifying scores for each branch are: Army: 31 Marine Corps: 31 Navy: 35 Air Force: 31 Coast Guard: 32 These scores apply to candi
dates with high school degrees: those with GEDs typically need a score of 50 or above to qualify. A military recruiter can provide you with the most accurate, up-to-date information on minim
um AFQT scores by branch. The remaining ASVAB subtests are more specialized and used to determine a candidate's suitability for different career areas within the military. Results on AFQT su
btests may also be used for career-matching purposes, as they represent baseline knowledge needed for a variety of fields. ASVAB exam summary Arithmetic Reasoning (AR) 27% - 29% 15 questions
