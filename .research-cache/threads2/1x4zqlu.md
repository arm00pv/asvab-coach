As an Army recruiter, I would honestly recommend reaching out to a recruiter now rather than waiting 90 days. There's nothing wrong with wanting to prepare before taking the ASVAB, but you don't have to do everything on your own.

I can't speak for every recruiter, but I personally work with applicants who need help improving their scores. I have a few websites and study resources I share with my applicants to help them prepare for the ASVAB.

We can also have you take a practice test to see where you're currently at. That way, instead of studying everything for 90 days, you can focus on the subjects you actually need to improve.

Also, keep in mind that the ASVAB isn't the only part of the enlistment process. There's medical history, paperwork, and other things we can work on while you're studying.

At the end of the day, a good recruiter should be helping you through the process, not just pressuring you to take a test before you're ready.

If you need some study resources, feel free to reach out. I'll gladly share the links I have. No pressure to join the Army either. Just happy to help someone who's trying to serve! 💪
