Stop cutting labels off, explain the jargon, and update over the air

Three things a real phone user runs into, in the order they run into them.

Nothing truncates any more. A test now walks the render tree of every screen at
phone, small-phone and tablet width and fails if any paragraph had to drop words,
which found real cuts in places that mattered: the dashboard stat suffix (/135
cut to /), the subtest rows, the plan and progress skill names, the test centre
subtest names, the MOS titles and their gate verdict (Qualified became Quali...),
the enlistment and officer requirement sentences, the search hint, the results
title, and the lesson title. Names wrap now, chips holding a requirement became a
bulleted list, and the runner's section card scrolls when enlarged text makes it
too tall. The test measures with real Roboto metrics, so it reports what a phone
shows rather than what the Ahem test font would.

The app explains itself. A glossary covers AFQT, VE, SS, GT and the ten line
scores, the nine subtests, MOS gates, PiCAT and Vtest, and the category bands, in
plain words, reachable from the dashboard, the report headings and Settings.

And it can update itself. Settings > Updates shows the installed build, checks
GitHub for a newer release at launch (switchable), and downloads and hands the
APK to Android's installer. Two permissions now exist and both are declared with
their reason: INTERNET for that check, REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES for the hand-off.
Nothing about the candidate is sent, and no attempt data ever leaves the device.

Also: the update lookup is detached from boot, because a platform-channel round
trip needs the real event loop and awaiting it inside init() made every widget
test hang for minutes.
