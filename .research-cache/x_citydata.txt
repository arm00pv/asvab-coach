That said, someone with a score like that will likely get a higher score on the the ASVAB. Reason is, it shows you know a little something, so the full test is more chances for you to get stuff right, versus someone who it may be more of a chance for them to get things wrong. That said, don't be cocky, which is typically a sure fire way to a lower score b/c you won't tkae the test seriously and it will make stupid mistakes. The asvab is full of trick questions. It's only written at a 10th grade level, but is is easy to make stupid mistakes if you're not paying attention. Conversely it is easy to luck up on a passing score if you have good "problem solving" skills even if you don't actually know the material. ALso, that high score doesn't neccesarily mean grat job. The only sections that go into that score are the 4 english and math sections. There are also electrical , mechanical, spatial reasoning, and general science sections. These sections will more heavily influence the number of jobs you qualify for. I've seen people with a 46 qualify for more jobs than someone with an 80.
