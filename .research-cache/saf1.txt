You son’s performance in an AFROTC program or at USAFA, the needs of the Air Force, what he learns about AF officer specialties through the academic year and summer training experiences, and how his preferences form - those will all come into play. Generally speaking, most officer candidates in any program don’t take the ASVAB, as it not designed for that purpose.

AFROTC scholarship selection uses essentially the same factors as the SAs to determine qualifications and officer potential.

My recollection is that ASVAB stands for Armed Services _Vocational Aptitude_ Battery.... the key words being vocational aptitude , or as CAPT MJ indicated, determining what technical specialty someone enlisting is suited for. While officer's need to be technically proficient in whatever position they are put in (and in some cases, specialize in technical fields), the ASVAB really doesn't apply to officer assignments. There is a aptitude test for aviation ; I forget the acryonym --something like AFQT (I've seen references on this Forum from time to time, and it may have a different name now), but that is given 2nd /1st Class year.
