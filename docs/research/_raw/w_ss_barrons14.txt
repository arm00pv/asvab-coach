## About the Book


**Be prepared for exam day with Barron’s—trusted test prep from experienced experts.**  
  
**Barron’s ASVAB Study Guide Premium, Fourteenth Edition** gives future recruits everything 
they need to succeed on the Armed Services Vocational Aptitude Battery with comprehensive review, strategic guidance, and extensive practice.  
  
**All the Review You Need to Be Prepared*
*  

*   Expert overview of the ASVAB and AFQT scoring
*   In-depth subject review covering every section of the test
*   New chapter exploring **Space Force and Cyber career opportuniti
es**
*   Strategic tips and insights from retired U.S. Army Lt. Col. Terry L. Duran

  
**Practice with Confidence**  

*   **7 full-length practice tests**—4 in the book and **4 onlin
e**—including a diagnostic test 
*   TWO focused Armed Forces Qualification Tests (AFQTs) that center on core requirements
*   **Hundreds of practice questions** throughout the book to rei
nforce key concepts
*   Detailed answer explanations for every question

  
**Interactive Learning Tools**  

*   Online practice tests with timed testing options and automated scoring


  
Whether you’re preparing to enlist in the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, or Space Force, Barron’s ASVAB Study Guide Premium helps you build the skills and confiden
ce needed to succeed.
