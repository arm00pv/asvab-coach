Title: Does the ASVAB have the same questions as the PICAT? - r/AirForceRecruits

URL Source: https://reddit.synth.download/r/AirForceRecruits/comments/1v5liyl/

Markdown Content:
The PiCAT is usually a test you take at home or a recruiter office then you go to meps to verify the score and that’s used as your ASVAB score.  
If you took the full PiCAT and got a score you like, once you get to meps, the test will be half same questions and half different.  
The questions that are the same pick the same exact answer you picked on the PiCAT and the ones that are new try your best.  
If you fail to verify your score it will enroll you into the full ASVAB and you’ll have to take the whole thing at meps

Now if it was just an actual practice test then non of this applies it’ll be some of the same types of questions
