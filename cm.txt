Prove the update path on a real phone, and fix what it found

Ran the updater end to end on a Pixel 7 Pro: the installed build said 1.1.9, the
launch check found the published 1.2.1, the dashboard offered it, the download
completed and the phone came out the other side on 1.2.1 with its history intact.

Three things that run turned up, all fixed here:

- A download that stopped early was handed straight to the installer, and Android
  answered it with a bare "App not installed". The expected size now travels with
  the request, a short file is deleted and the real reason is reported.
- With Termux installed, the update went through an Open with chooser. The
  updater now asks for the system package installer by name and falls back to the
  chooser only when there is none.
- The settings screen told an Android user their history was stored in this
  browser only, and said 1 attempts.

Play Protect also appears on the way through for anything installed outside the
Play Store; the update card says so now instead of leaving the candidate to guess
whether something is wrong.
