# v1.10.0 - the voice starts talking sooner, and you can pick which one

Two refinements to the downloaded voices.

* **First sound in about a second.** An answer used to be synthesised in one
  call and played afterwards: four seconds of silence on a Pixel before a word
  was heard. The text is now split at sentence boundaries and each sentence is
  spoken as soon as it is made, so the first one plays while the rest is still
  being synthesised. Nothing is dropped - joining the pieces gives back exactly
  what the screen shows - and a very long sentence is broken at a word boundary
  so it cannot hold the first sound hostage.
* **Eleven English voices to choose from.** The Kokoro pack has eleven speakers;
  Settings now lists them (Heart, Bella, Nicole, Sarah, Sky, Adam, Michael,
  Emma, Isabella, George, Lewis) once the English pack is installed, and the
  choice is remembered. *Hear it* speaks with the one you picked.

No new download, no new permission, no change to the APK's native code.

Also verified on the phone this round: the Italian pack downloads, checks,
unpacks and speaks, and the Spanish and English packs still do.

Nothing about scoring, the job catalogue or the question bank changed.
