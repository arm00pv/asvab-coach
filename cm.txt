Verify an update against the SHA-256 GitHub publishes

The updater already refused a download that stopped short, by size. Size alone
cannot tell a truncated file from a rewritten one, and GitHub publishes a SHA-256
for every release asset, so the check now carries that through: the digest is read
from the releases API, hashed locally after the download and compared, and a file
that does not match is deleted rather than handed to the installer. When a release
carries no digest the check falls back to size alone instead of refusing to
update.

Tested through the channel with the digest present and absent, and proven on
device: a phone on 1.2.1 found the published 1.2.2 at launch and offered it.

Also says in the update card that Play Protect may offer to scan the app, which
it does for anything installed outside the Play Store.
