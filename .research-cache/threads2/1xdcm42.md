I shared Brain Preflight on [r/ArmyAviationApplicant](https://reddit.synth.download/r/ArmyAviationApplicant) recently for people preparing for the SIFT. I thought the timed math exercises might also be useful here for anyone brushing up on arithmetic before the ASVAB.

One exercise gives you a grid of calculations and asks you to spot the wrong answers before time runs out. You can also practise number sequences and work on spatial reasoning with rotating 3D objects and cube nets.

I’m the developer. The app started with pilot selection practice, so it’s built around short exercises under a timer. You can choose individual exercises and track your progress.

**Heads up: some exercises are genuinely hard. The aim is to practice under pressure, so they won’t all feel like casual phone games.** These aren’t official ASVAB questions. It’s extra arithmetic practice alongside your study material, rather than a full ASVAB course or a prediction of your score.

5 exercise sessions a day for free, everyday

[Brain Preflight on iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6794363864?pt=129217861&ct=reddit-asvab&mt=8)

For anyone studying the math sections: is the bigger problem knowing how to solve the question, or doing it quickly enough without a calculator?
