It's a broad measurement of education and training. It's comparable to the SAT or ACT. The difficulty ranges from easy to advanced within the test. It measures where you fall in that range.

I highly recommend checking out an ASVAB test book from the library. Take your time and do the exercises cover to cover. The better you score on the test, the more options you have when choosing an MOS. That's something you can do now, before even talking to a recruiter.

I took the ASVAB when I joined the Army. It’s a lot like the SAT that colleges use for admission. It has a little more mechanical stuff on it than the SAT, such as a few basics about automobiles and so forth. It’s not an IQ test where you have to figure out complicated puzzles. If you paid attention in high school, you will do fine.

It emphasizes math and science. For instance, I remember one question was, “What do the three inner angles of a triangle equal?” Answer: They always equal 180 degrees. An orange is what kind of fruit? Answer: citrus. There will be nothing on it about literature, music, history, politics, poetry, art, philosophy, or drama because soldiers do not need to know anything about those things.

Keep in mind that you will not be competing with people trying to get into college. You will be competing with people trying to get into the military. Most people pass quite easily.

The ASVAB is not hard per se. It is simply a means of evaluating the intrinsic affinities of the potential enlistee in determining the more suitable military occupation specialties (MOS). The ASVAB is not perfect but provides at least a crude baseline from which to proceed in MOS selection. Some MOS’s require a certain score in particular ASVAB sections in order to qualify for training in them.

What are some tips to pass the ASVAB?

ASVAB is not a test but an assessment to show your strengths and no so strong skills. The ASVAB is broken down into nine sections. They are Arithmetic Reasoning (AR), Assembling Objects (AO), Auto and Shop Information (AS), Electronics Information (EI), General Science (GS), Mathematics Knowledge (MK), Mechanical Comprehension (MC), Paragraph Comprehension (PC), and Word Knowledge (WK). It is used to help place you into a career field you will succeed in. So there is no need to study or be apprehensive.

1.   There is no pass or fail on the ASVAB. The test is an assement of skils.
2.   Before you take the test, know what job you are working for and know what sections you need to do well on. Let me explain further. The ASVAB is broken down into areas and there are ten of them.

*   Arithmetic Reasoning (AR)
*   Word Knowledge (WK)
*   Paragraph Comprehension (PC)
*   Mathematics Knowledge (MK)
*   General Science (GS)
*   Electronics Information (EI)
*   Auto Information (AI)
*   Shop Information (SI)
*   Mechanical Comprehension (MC)
*   Assembling Objects (AO)

3. Every branch has a minimum score that need to be met to enter the service. Know what makes that score up. AFQT

4. Your job that you qualify for will be made up of some combination of the above sections and determines if you are eligible for that job.

General Science (GS), Arithmetic Reasoning (AR), Mathematics Knowledge (MK), Electronics Information (EI)
