Let the jobs screen be filtered by type: enlisted, officer, and career field

The catalogue could be searched and grouped by the Army's coarse combat /
combat-support categories, which answers a question nobody asks. A candidate
either wants an enlisted job or a commission, and then a kind of work.

The career field now comes from the MOS code itself - every 68 is medical, every
25 is signal, every 12 is engineer - so ninety-seven jobs are grouped without
hand-labelling them, and a test fails if a job ever arrives without a field or if
a filter would render empty.

Officer routes are grouped by how they are taken rather than what they
commission into: officer candidate school, college programme, service academy,
direct commission, warrant officer, and enlist-then-commission. That mapping is
explicit and a test fails when a route is added without one, because a route
filtered out of existence is a route a candidate never sees.

The filter bar reads type first, then field or track, and the officer section
shows only the routes the chips asked for.
