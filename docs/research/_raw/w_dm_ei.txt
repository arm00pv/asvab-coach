s a current completes two alternations of direction per second is known as its frequency; the unit of measurement for frequency is the hertz (Hz). Electronic devices operate at very high fre
quencies. Electronic devices often require a specific capacitive or inductive reactance to work. Capacitors and inductors are devices used in circuits to provide the type of reactance needed
