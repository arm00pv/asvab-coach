We looked at **60,534**ASVAB practice answers from**585** people using ASVAB Hero. Across all included answers, test-takers answered**62.6%** correctly. Mechanical Comprehension had the lowest correct rate, while Paragraph Comprehension had the highest.

## Key findings

*   Mechanical Comprehension is the hardest subtest at 46.8% correct.
*   Series & Parallel Circuits is the hardest qualifying topic at 45.2% correct.
*   Mechanical and electronics topics account for 9 of the 12 hardest topics.
*   Paragraph Comprehension is the easiest subtest at 79.4%, a 32.6-point gap from Mechanical Comprehension.
*   Geometry: Area, Perimeter & Volume is the hardest qualifying math topic at 54.6% correct.

## What this means for your study time

The results point to three practical priorities: mechanical concepts, electrical concepts, and geometry.

**Start with circuits and mechanical concepts.**Nine of the 12 hardest qualifying topics come from Mechanical Comprehension or Electronics Information. The list begins with[Series & Parallel Circuits](https://asvabhero.com/study/ei/circuit-types)at 45.2% correct, followed by[Gears, Wheels & Axles](https://asvabhero.com/study/mc/gears-wheels)at 45.3%. See the full[electronics tips](https://asvabhero.com/asvab-electronics-information-tips)and[mechanical comprehension tips](https://asvabhero.com/asvab-mechanical-comprehension-tips).

**Do not treat every AFQT section as easy.**The four subtests that decide whether you can enlist at all (Arithmetic Reasoning, Word Knowledge, Paragraph Comprehension, and Mathematics Knowledge) span the ranking. Mathematics Knowledge is fourth hardest at 58.0%, Arithmetic Reasoning is fifth at 61.0%, Word Knowledge is eighth at 73.4%, and Paragraph Comprehension is ninth at 79.4%.

**Within math, geometry ranks hardest.**The hardest qualifying AR or MK topic is[geometry](https://asvabhero.com/study/mk/geometry)at 54.6% correct, followed by Number Properties & Absolute Value at 55.9% and Exponents & Polynomials at 56.5%.

To put these scores in context, see the[ASVAB score ranges by branch](https://asvabhero.com/asvab-score-ranges)for the minimum AFQT each service requires.

## Methodology

*   **Sample:**60,534 practice-question answers from 585 people using ASVAB Hero from May 6 through September 16, 2026. The query window begins April 28, and May 6 is the date of the first included answer. Percent correct is answers marked correct divided by answers attempted. Questions left blank count as attempted and incorrect, which lowers every rate: Arithmetic Reasoning is 61.0% correct with blanks included and 70.7% on answered questions alone.
*   **Exclusions:** internal and owner test accounts are excluded, as are remediation entries shown after a question reveal.
*   **Scope:** topic-level figures include only topics with at least 60 answers. Every question is four-choice, so 25% is the pure-guessing baseline.
*   **What this is not:** these are practice-question results on ASVAB Hero, not official ASVAB scores. The people here chose to study with a prep tool, so they are not a random sample of all test-takers, and adaptive practice can serve harder questions to stronger test-takers. Read the rankings as a signal of relative difficulty, not an official statistic.
*   **What changed in edition 2:**the sample grew from 12,368 to 60,534 answers. Edition 2 excludes internal test accounts, which edition 1 included, and Mechanical Comprehension replaced Assembling Objects as the hardest subtest.
*   **Study by topic:**Our[ASVAB study guide](https://asvabhero.com/asvab-study-guide)covers each subtest and the hard topics in this list with targeted practice.
